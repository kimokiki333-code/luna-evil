Arabic subtitle generator for reels and short videos

BaDR Video Studio is designed for short English-language clips that need Arabic captions. The workflow combines speech recognition, translation and video rendering so you can upload a video and receive a finished subtitled MP4 without manually creating an English transcript first. Processing speed depends on your configured machine, Whisper model and available CPU/GPU resources.
